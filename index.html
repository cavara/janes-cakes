Welcome

Handmade cakes for every celebration

Over the last 5 years, we have helped residents of Surrey to have memorable experiences using our unique cake recipes.

We have recently decided to make this service which was previously available through word of mouth, available to everyone across the UK.

Whether it's a birthday, wedding, baby shower or just because, I'd love to create something special for you. Every design is made to match your style, your colours and your celebration.
