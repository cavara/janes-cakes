About Me

Hi, I'm Jane!

I've been baking for over ten years, and nothing makes me happier than seeing someone's face light up when their cake arrives. What started as birthday cakes for friends and family has grown into a little business I'm so proud of.

Every cake is baked fresh to order in my kitchen using free-range eggs, real butter and the best ingredients I can find. From light, fluffy sponges to rich chocolate layers, I put the same care into every bake.

Whether it's a birthday, wedding, baby shower or just because, I'd love to create something special for you. Every design is made to match your style, your colours and your celebration.

My baking journey

From a wobbly first birthday cake to a business of my own.

  1. 2014

    My first proper cake

    A three-layer rainbow cake for my niece's 5th birthday. It leaned a little, but her face when she saw it got me hooked.

  2. 2016

    Baking for friends and family

    Word spread quickly, and I was soon baking birthday and christening cakes for friends, family and neighbours most weekends.

  3. 2019

    Qualified and registered

    I completed my Level 2 Food Hygiene certificate and registered my home kitchen with the local council.

  4. 2021

    Jane's Cakes is born

    I officially launched Jane's Cakes in Horley, taking orders from customers across Surrey.

  5. 2023

    My first wedding cake

    A three-tier floral wedding cake for a couple in Reigate. Weddings are now one of my favourite things to bake.

  6. 2026

    Jane's Cakes goes online

    This website launched, making it easier than ever to browse my cakes and place an order.
