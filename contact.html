Delivery & Collection

Getting your cake safely from my kitchen to your celebration matters as much as baking it.

Collection

Free collection from my kitchen in Horley, Surrey, at a time that suits you. I'll confirm the address and a collection slot when you order.

Local delivery

I deliver within 15 miles of Horley, including Crawley, Reigate, Redhill and Gatwick. Delivery costs £10, and is free on orders over £75.

Wedding and tiered cakes

Tiered and wedding cakes are always delivered and set up by me at your venue, so they arrive looking perfect. Further afield? Just ask.

How far ahead to order

At least 2 weeks for celebration cakes, and 3 to 6 months for wedding cakes. Short notice? Get in touch and I'll do my best.

Travelling with your cake

Every cake comes in a sturdy box. Carry it flat in the car footwell or boot, never on a seat, keep the car cool, and drive gently round corners.

Allergies and dietary needs

Gluten-free, dairy-free and vegan options are available. My kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs, so please mention any allergies when you order.
